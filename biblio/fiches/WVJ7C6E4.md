# Enhanced multi-class object detector for bone fracture diagnosis with prescription recommendation.

**Auteurs** : Migayo DM, Kaijage S, Swetala S, Nyambo DG.
**Année** : 2025
**DOI** : 10.3389/frai.2025.1692894

## Résumé

Bone fractures are among the most prominent injuries in the modern world that affect all ages and races. Traditional treatment involves radiographic imaging that relies heavily on radiologists manually analyzing images. There have been efforts to develop computer-aided diagnosis tools that employ artificial intelligence and deep learning approaches. Existing literature focuses on developing tools that only detect and classify bone fractures, rather than addressing the broader issue of bone fracture management. However, evidence of scholarly works that include treatment recommendations is still lacking. Furthermore, deep learning-based object detectors that achieve state-of-the-art results are computationally expensive and considered as black-box solutions.
