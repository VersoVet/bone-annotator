# Joint aortic root segmentation and landmark localization on intraoperative fluoroscopy for TAVI guidance.

**Auteurs** : Nikita V Laptev, Olga M Gerget, Julia K Panteleeva, Mikhail A Chernyavsky, Viacheslav V Danilov
**Année** : 2026
**DOI** : 10.3389/fcvm.2026.1886469

## Résumé

Accurate intraoperative delineation of the aortic root and localization of key anatomical landmarks during transcatheter aortic valve implantation (TAVI) remain difficult because fluoroscopy provides low soft-tissue contrast and is frequently degraded by motion and overlap from catheters and delivery systems. This study developed and validated a multitask deep learning model for simultaneous aortic root segmentation and landmark localization on fluoroscopic images to support image-guided TAVI. A retrospective dataset of 2,895 fully anonymized fluoroscopic frames from 83 patients who underwent TAVI between 2018 and 2024 was used. Expert annotations included binary masks of the contrast-enhanced aortic root and four anatomical landmarks: two aortic annulus points (AA1, AA2) and two sinotubular junction points near the coronary ostia (STJ1, STJ2).
