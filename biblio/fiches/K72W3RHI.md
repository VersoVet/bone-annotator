# Transfer Learning from an Artificial Radiograph-landmark Dataset for Registration of the Anatomic Skull Model to Dual Fluoroscopic X-ray Images

**Auteurs** : Chaochao Zhou, Thomas Cha, Yun Peng, Guoan Li
**Année** : 2021
**DOI** : 10.1016/j.compbiomed.2021.104923

## Résumé

Registration of 3D anatomic structures to their 2D dual fluoroscopic X-ray images is a widely used motion tracking technique. However, deep learning implementation is often impeded by a paucity of medical images and ground truths. In this study, we proposed a transfer learning strategy for 3D-to-2D registration using deep neural networks trained from an artificial dataset. Digitally reconstructed radiographs (DRRs) and radiographic skull landmarks were automatically created from craniocervical CT data of a female subject.
