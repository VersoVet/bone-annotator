# BDD100K: A Diverse Driving Video Database with Scalable Annotation Tooling.



## Methodologie


L’article présente BDD100K, un jeu de données à grande échelle conçu pour pallier les limites des corpus existants de conduite autonome en matière de diversité des scènes, de richesse des annotations et de couverture géographique. La base comprend plus de 100 000 séquences vidéo de conduite et vise à mieux représenter les situations réalistes ainsi que les variations rares d’apparence et de configuration des objets. Sa constitution repose sur un système d’annotation configurable prenant en charge l’étiquetage au niveau de l’image, les boîtes englobantes et les polygones, puis étendu notamment aux voies, aux zones carrossables et à la segmentation d’instances. Les auteurs revendiquent ainsi une double contribution : un corpus diversifié destiné à l’évaluation des méthodes de vision pour la conduite autonome et un dispositif d’annotation à la fois efficace, extensible et adapté au passage à l’échelle [Unknown](zotero://select/library/items/DVCH9333).

## Raw_Labeling_System


Our goal is to design a versatile and scalable annotation tooling that is suitable for all kinds of annotations needed in a driving database, such as bounding box, semantic instance segmentation, and lane detection. Many open source annotation tools are targeted to one specific task, such as single object classification or vehicle/pedestrian detection, however, no existing open source tool is available to support various types of annotations for such a large driving database. Also, in order to have an extensive collection of images annotated with maximum efficiency, the annotation work must be easily accessible to workers, the annotation progress needs to be monitorable, and concurrent annotation sessions need to be supported.

Our goal is to design a versatile and scalable annotation tooling that is suitable for all kinds of annotations needed in a driving database, such as bounding box, semantic instance segmentation, and lane detection. Many open source annotation tools are targeted to one specific task, such as single object classification or vehicle/pedestrian detection, however, no existing open source tool is available to support various types of annotations for such a large driving database. Also, in order to have an extensive collection of images annotated with maximum efficiency, the annotation work must be easily accessible to workers, the annotation progress needs to be monitorable, and concurrent annotation sessions need to be supported.

To satisfy these requirements, we design an efficient labeling system that is sufficient for the annotation of a large driving video database. First of all, the system is adaptable so that it would allow the practice of various kinds of annotation work such as bounding box and region annotation. Secondly, the system aims to make the annotation process as seamless and user-friendly as possible.
