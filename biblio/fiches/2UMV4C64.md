# TU-DAT: A Computer Vision Dataset on Road Traffic Anomalies.

**Auteurs** : Pradeep Kumar P, Kant K.
**Année** : 2025
**DOI** : 10.3390/s25113259

## Résumé

This paper introduces TU-DAT, a novel, freely downloadable computer vision dataset for analyzing traffic accidents using roadside cameras. TU-DAT addresses the lack of public datasets for training and evaluating models focused on automatic detection and prediction of road anomalies. It comprises approximately 280 real-world and simulated videos, collected from traffic CCTV footage, news reports, and high-fidelity simulations generated using BeamNG.drive. This hybrid composition captures aggressive driving behaviors-such as tailgating, weaving, and speeding-under diverse environmental conditions. It includes spatiotemporal annotations and structured metadata such as vehicle trajectories, collision types, and road conditions. These features enable robust model training for anomaly detection, spatial reasoning, and vision-language model (VLM) enhancement.
